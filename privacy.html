Privacy Policy

VignetteMuseum · Last updated November 8, 2026

VignetteMuseum does not collect, store or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer, Evrim Erdil, runs no server and cannot see your collection.

What stays on your device

Your collections, pieces, notes, purchase dates, prices and photos are stored on your device. They are never sent to the developer.

Photos and camera

You choose which photos to add from your photo library, or take one with the camera. The app analyzes them on your device with Apple’s Vision framework to remove the background, pick card colors and read printed text. Nothing is uploaded for this. The app saves to your photo library only when you ask it to export a card image.

Motion sensors

On iPhone and iPad, the app reads device motion so cards can tilt as you move your device. This data is used live on the device and is not recorded or sent anywhere.

iCloud sync

Sync is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, your data is stored in your own private iCloud storage through Apple’s CloudKit, under the Apple Account signed in on your device. Apple manages that storage under its own privacy policy, and the developer has no access to it. Turning sync off stops future syncing but does not remove copies already stored in iCloud.

Files you export, back up or share

The app creates .vignette files only for features you use: backups, exports and sharing. A setting in Sync Settings can also keep an automatic backup of your collection as a .vignette file in iCloud Drive or your Documents folder. It is off by default. When you turn it on, the backup is complete: it includes your photos, notes, purchase dates and prices. Backups you create yourself with “Back Up Museum to File…” are complete in the same way. When you export to share with another collector, purchase date and price are left out unless you choose to include them. Once you send a file to someone, it is outside the app’s control.

Third parties

VignetteMuseum contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and shares no data with any third party.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

You can delete any piece or collection in the app, and deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. Data you synced to iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
